Works council

A works council is a group of people who represent the workers of an organization The committee will be made up of a president, a secretary and a specific number of members, which will depend on the number of workers in the company. Among the main functions of a works council are the representation of the workers and the negotiation with the employer of different aspects related to the labor rights of the employees.

The structure of the works council will be the following:

The chairman of the committee

His functions will include, for example, chairing the meetings, ensuring compliance with the committee's regulations, moderating the debates that take place during the assemblies and signing the minutes. He will also take care of bringing the proposals to a vote. In addition, the president is the one who will legally represent the committee before public administrations, individuals and legal entities.

The secretary of the committee

The secretary will be in charge of supporting the president in carrying out his functions

The other members of the committee

whose number will depend on the total number of workers that the company has. These members will appear on the lists and will be democratically elected by the company's workers.

All those workers who are at least 18 years old and have been in the company for at least 6 months will be eligible to become members of the works council. In some cases, this period may be less if so established by the applicable collective agreement. On the other hand, all company workers who are over 16 years of age and whose seniority in the company is at least one month may participate in the election process for the members of this committee.

What are the functions of the works council?

The main objective of a works council is, in general terms, to collectively represent the workers. This group fulfills a series of administrative and informational functions, and also deals with collective bargaining.

This collective bargaining includes a series of aspects such as the working conditions of the workers, overtime, working hours, working days, different measures taken by the company,... The negotiation is carried out between the committee of company and businessman. After these negotiations or meetings, it will be the committee delegate who will be in charge of informing the employees about the result of the meeting or the conclusions reached.

Other functions of the works council may include supervision of the code of conduct and business ethics; inform the team of workers of different aspects that affect professional relations; ensure that labor standards are met; carry out surveillance in relation to occupational risk prevention and occupational health, and ensure that there is equality at work
