Coffe Torero

Los Angeles

Description

Founded in 2008 by Diego Castilla, Mario Reyes and Sergio Corruchaga in Los Angeles, California. The CoffeTorero® brand has grown to become the second largest coffee shop chain in United States.

At Coffe Torero we dream big, we have a clear vision and the goal of being the largest and most profitable coffee shop company in Southern EE.UU, with the best people and offering a special customer experience.

One of the keys to our success lies in operational excellence that can only be achieved with the best team. Our vision is to create a stable team, with an environment that encourages training and professional development.

If you are an ambitious person who wants to develop your professional career in a dynamic environment, Coffe Torero is your place!!!
